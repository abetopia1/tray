## Wording choices

- Line 6 says "suspected". The 29 October report described surveillance of a suspected asset. The claim that China's Ministry of State Security recruited her came in later Times reporting.
- Line 7 says "He names no one". Huang's email admitted infidelity without naming Alice Huang or Megaspeed. Outlets connect the two; he did not.
- Line 8 quotes only "absolutely no idea". Trump's full sentence calls her "a spy", which is an unproven allegation.
- Line 9 "no one has been charged" is true of every report read as of 11 November 2026. No outlet states it as a standalone fact, so the on-screen stamp carries the date.
